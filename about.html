bio

Trevor is the former host of The Daily Show, the author of the #1New York Times bestsellers Born a Crime and Into the Uncut Grass, and host of the weekly Sirius XM podcast What Now? with Trevor Noah. He is also the founder of Day Zero Productions and the Trevor Noah Foundation.

Day Zero

Productions

Day Zero Productions is an Emmy-nominated production company creating film and television content for a global audience.

From major adaptations like Born a Crime and Long Division to original films such as The Incomer, which premiered at Sundance 2026, its work spans formats and genres, including unscripted projects ranging from the documentary TUTU to series with partners including Amazon, Showtime, and HBO Max.

The company is also currently expanding into gaming with projects like Fortnite’s JokeNite.

The Trevor Noah

Foundation

Founded in 2018 in South Africa by Trevor Noah, the Trevor Noah Foundation is rooted in the belief that education is the seed from which everything grows.

The Foundation provides young people across Africa with access to quality education, supported teachers, and clear employment pathways, equipping them to lead and drive economic transformation.

Through its flagship Khulani Schools Program, the Foundation partners
with schools to create stronger learning environments, reaching more than 13,000 students and 500 educators annually.

Its Education Changemakers initiative develops the next generation of education leaders, while the Khulani Nathi Innovation Fund provides flexible funding to nonprofits pioneering new approaches to learning.
